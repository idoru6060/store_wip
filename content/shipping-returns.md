## Shipping

- Orders ship the next business day after they're placed.
- We send a tracking number by email as soon as your order is on its way.
- If an order is ever delayed, we'll let you know by email.

## Returns & refunds

We want you to be happy with what you buy. If something isn't right, email us

refund.
