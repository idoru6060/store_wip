We started this shop with a simple idea: everyday things — a mug, a candle, a
tote — are better when they're made carefully, by people who care, from
materials that last.

Everything in the store is designed in-house and made in small batches. We
don't chase trends or seasons; we make a few things well, restock them when
they sell out, and retire them only when we've designed something genuinely
better.

## How we work

- **Small batches.** Nothing is mass-produced. If something is sold out, it's
  because we're making more.
- **Honest materials.** Stoneware, soy wax, natural canvas — things that age
  well and can be used every day.
- **No middlemen.** We sell directly to you, which keeps prices fair and lets
  us answer for every item that leaves the studio.
