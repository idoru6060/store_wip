We keep this simple: we only collect what we need to take your order and get
it to you.

## What we collect

- **Order details** — items, quantities, and total, so we can fulfill your order.
- **Your email address** — to send order confirmations and tracking numbers.
- **Shipping address** — to deliver your order (collected by Stripe at checkout).

## Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. Stripe
provides us with your name, email, and shipping address to complete the order.

## How we use it

We use your information only to fulfill your order and respond to you. We don't
sell it, and we don't send marketing emails unless you ask us to.
